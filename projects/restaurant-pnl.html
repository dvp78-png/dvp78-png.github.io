Restaurant P&L & Margin Analyzer

An interactive profit-and-loss model for a small restaurant, based on the kind of Excel analysis I use as Head of Finances at a family-owned restaurant. Enter a monthly cost structure, then test cost-saving and pricing scenarios to see the effect on net margin, prime cost and break-even revenue.

Default figures are illustrative sample data, not actual business results. Model assumptions: the scenario keeps customer volume and labor dollars constant; a price increase raises revenue without raising food cost dollars; break-even revenue = fixed costs ÷ (1 − food % − labor %). Net income is before income taxes.
